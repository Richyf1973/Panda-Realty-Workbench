# Buyer & Seller Portal Uploads — Questions Before We Build

Hi Richard,

Before we start building the ability for buyers and sellers to upload their own files through the portal, we need your input on a few decisions. Please reply with the letter for each question — e.g. "1B, 2A, 3C".

## Background

Right now, the buyer portal and seller portal are read-only for clients — your team uploads files in the admin panel and then switches on a "Share" toggle to make them visible. We're adding the ability for buyers and sellers to upload files themselves directly through their portal. Those uploaded files would then appear in the admin panel for your team to view.

## Questions

   A. Nobody — agents check the portal at their own pace — Less noise for the team
   B. Only the agent assigned to that deal gets a notification — Right person alerted right away
   C. The whole team sees a notification — Nothing gets missed

2. **After a buyer or seller uploads a file, can the other party see it through their portal?**

   A. No — buyer uploads are visible only to your team, and seller uploads are visible only to your team — Full privacy between parties
   B. Yes — buyers can see what sellers uploaded and sellers can see what buyers uploaded — Full transparency between parties

3. **Can buyers and sellers delete files they uploaded themselves?**

   A. Yes — they can remove their own uploads at any time — Less work for your team to clean up
   B. No — once uploaded it stays; only your team can remove it — Your team stays in control of the record

5. **Should there be a size limit per file that a buyer or seller uploads?**

6. **When your team views the admin panel, where should buyer/seller uploads appear?**

   A. In the same Files tab your team already uses, clearly labelled "Uploaded by buyer" or "Uploaded by seller" — One place for all files
   B. In a separate new section so agent uploads and client uploads are always visually separated — Clean split

7. **Should buyers be able to upload files, sellers be able to upload files, or both?**

   A. Both buyers and sellers can upload — Full two-way capability
   B. Buyers only — Sellers remain read-only for now
   C. Sellers only — Buyers remain read-only for now
