## Background

Right now, when a property has multiple owners, each owner's phone and email field has a little star icon. Clicking the star marks that owner as the "primary" contact for phone or email — the one agents should call or email first. The buyer section is missing this star button on the phone and email fields, and you'd like it added to match.

Before I build it, I need to know exactly what "primary buyer" means and what it affects.

---

## Questions

1. **When a property has multiple buyers, can the primary phone and primary email be different buyers?**

   For example: Buyer 1 is the best person to call, but Buyer 2 handles all the emails.

   A. Yes — track primary phone and primary email separately (one star per field, just like the owner section) — *Most flexible*
   B. No — one buyer is the primary contact for everything (one star marks the whole buyer as primary) — *Simpler, one decision*

2. **When an agent clicks the call or email action buttons in the buyer row, what should happen?**

   The buyer row already has action icons (call, email, SMS, etc.) at the bottom. Does the star affect those buttons?

   A. Yes — clicking the call button should pre-fill the primary buyer's phone number, and email button should pre-fill the primary buyer's email — *Star has real effect on workflow*
   B. No — the star is purely a visual marker so agents know at a glance who to contact; the action buttons keep working exactly as they do today — *Visual only, no behaviour change*

3. **Should the star show up even when there is only one buyer?**

   A. Yes — always show it for consistency, even if there is only one buyer — *Uniform look across all properties*
   B. No — only show the star when there are two or more buyers, since it has no purpose with a single buyer — *Cleaner UI for single-buyer properties*

4. **Which stages of a property should have the star?**

   Properties go through different stages: listed but no offer yet, offer accepted, settled/closed.

   A. All stages — always show the star on buyer phone and email fields regardless of where the property is in the process — *Consistent everywhere*
   B. Only while an offer is in progress (after an offer is accepted but before settlement) — *Only when it matters most*
   C. Only on active/listed properties (before an offer is accepted) — *Early-stage focus*
